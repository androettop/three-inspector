# Privacy Policy

Three Inspector does not collect, store or transmit any data.

- It requests no browser permissions.
- It makes no network requests and contains no analytics or tracking.
- It runs only inside Chrome DevTools, on the page being inspected, and only while its panel is open.
- Scene data read from the page (object names, properties, geometry) stays in the DevTools panel. Exported files are saved locally through the browser's download flow.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/androettop/three-inspector/issues.
